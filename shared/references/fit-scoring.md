# Fit Scoring Contract

Score every role on a 100-point scale:

| Dimension | Weight | Rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Hard eligibility | 30 | Degree, cohort, major, language and location constraints |
| Core capability | 35 | Direct evidence for required technical work |
| Engineering delivery | 20 | Deployment, evaluation, reliability and collaboration |
| Domain relevance | 10 | Industry or business scenario overlap |
| Preference fit | 5 | Candidate priorities |

Apply a hard-stop label when an explicit non-negotiable requirement is unmet. Separate `evidence`, `inference`, `gap`, and `unknown`. A high score never overrides a hard stop.
