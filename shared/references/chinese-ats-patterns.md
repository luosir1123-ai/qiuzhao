# Chinese ATS Patterns

Common platforms include Moka, Beisen/Zhiye, Feishu Recruiting, Dayee and employer-built portals. Field labels vary, so match by semantic meaning rather than DOM position.

Typical sections: personal information, application intent, education, internship, employment, project, awards, language, certificates, attachments and open questions.

Campus forms often distinguish internship from employment and project experience. Preserve the source category exactly. Do not move a project into internship unless the candidate performed it as part of that internship.
