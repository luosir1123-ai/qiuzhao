# Interview Pack

1. Positioning: a 60-second evidence-backed introduction.
2. Priority matrix: question, likelihood, consequence, evidence source.
3. Project deep dives: architecture, decisions, alternatives, failures, metrics, personal contribution.
4. Fundamentals: role-specific concepts and concise answer checkpoints.
5. Behavioral stories: STAR outlines with likely follow-ups.
6. Gap repair: honest answer and shortest validation exercise.
7. Candidate questions: team, evaluation, deployment, data, ownership and success metrics.
